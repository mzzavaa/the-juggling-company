# Community Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in this project. Before anything else, the important context: some of these repositories are public because that is how the site they hold gets published, and all of them are maintained by one person, in her own time, at her own cost. That spirit shapes how you can get involved.

## I would love to hear from you

The best thing you can do is reach out and talk to me. Ask a question, tell me what is wrong, suggest a change, share how you are using this, or just say hello. I genuinely want the conversation, and it is the way I most enjoy people getting involved.

- **[Message me on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/linda-mohamed/)**, for questions, ideas or feedback
- **[lindamohamed.com](https://www.lindamohamed.com)**, for consulting, speaking, workshops and advisory

## Why I am not taking direct contributions right now

I am the only maintainer, and this is my own work rather than a staffed project. If everyone who wanted to help opened a pull request, I would not be able to review them with the care the work deserves, and the accuracy I care about would slip. So for now I keep the editing in one pair of hands. This keeps things coherent and lets me stand behind what is here.

**Please do not open a pull request without talking to me first.** Unsolicited pull requests will most likely be closed, with thanks and no hard feelings. This is not about the quality of your work. It is about what one person can realistically keep up with.

If there is something specific you would love to contribute, reach out first and let us talk about it. I am open to it. I just want it to start as a conversation rather than a queue I cannot keep up with.

## Found an error?

This I always want to know about, because accuracy is the whole point. A wrong date, a misattributed source, a broken link, an outdated tool name: please tell me, through any of the channels above.

A source for the correction is very welcome, so I can verify and fix it quickly.

## Using and forking

You are welcome to read, share and build on this under its licence. If you fork it and make something good, I would love to hear about that too.

## A note on tone

Everything here aims to be accurate, sourced and free of marketing language. If you reach out about it, that is the bar I am holding it to, and the kind of feedback that helps most.

Thank you for being here.

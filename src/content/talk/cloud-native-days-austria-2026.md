AI systems amplify the patterns in their training data rather than reflecting reality, so when the data shows tech as male-dominated, models treat 'man' as the default for technical roles - across image generation, assistants, recommendations, hiring tools, and copilots, even when the headline accuracy metrics look fine. A look at the mechanism behind bias that is correct on average and wrong by design."
